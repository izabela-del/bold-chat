Frequently asked questions

Will Medicare cover my visit?

Yes — Original Medicare Part B covers telehealth visits from home, and most Medicare Advantage plans do too. 78% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket; if there's a cost, it's typically $5–$55 per visit.

How much do GLP-1 medications cost?

If a GLP-1 is right for you and you're eligible, it's a flat $50/month through the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge. A care coordinator confirms your exact cost for free before anything is prescribed.

Do I have to take medication?

Not at all. Bold focuses on nutrition, movement, sleep, and daily habits. You and your provider decide together whether medication is the right fit.

Is my information private and secure?

Yes. Bold is HIPAA compliant. Your health information is encrypted, never sold, and shared only with your care team.

Who are the providers?

Bold Care's board-certified providers specialize in weight loss, geriatrics, and lifestyle medicine, with years of experience caring for adults 65+.

How long does it take to get started?

Checking your coverage takes about 2 minutes. Then you book a free 15-minute consultation with a Bold care coordinator — a real person who confirms your coverage and exact costs, answers your questions, and schedules your first provider visit.
